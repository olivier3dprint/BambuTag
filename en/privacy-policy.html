Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

In short: Bambu Tag has no account and no server: what you read and write on your tags stays on your phone. The app does show ads provided by Google AdMob, which involve Google collecting an advertising ID. That collection only concerns ad delivery, never your tags or your content.

1. Publisher

Bambu Tag is developed and published by an independent developer, Olivier. Contact: olivier3dprint@gmail.com.

2. Data processed on your phone

To work, the app processes the following data on your device only:

  • Tag content: identifier (UID) and sectors read from a tag, content you write (links, text, Wi-Fi networks, business cards, positions, apps).
  • Remembered content: each successfully written content is kept in the app's private storage so you can reload it later. Wi-Fi passwords are encrypted with Android's secure storage.
  • Settings: theme and language.
  • Diagnostic log (off by default): when turned on in Options → Diagnostics, it records the details of reads and writes: raw data of the tags read (including any Wi-Fi password written on a tag), NFC steps and errors, phone model, Android version and app version. It is kept in memory only: it is cleared when the app closes and is never sent automatically. It only leaves the phone if you choose to share or copy it, for example to send it to the developer to solve a problem.

None of this data is sent automatically to a server, to the developer or to any third party: only a diagnostic log that you share yourself leaves the phone, to the recipient of your choice. The app contains no user account, no analytics and no external crash reporting. The “Clear remembered content” button in the options, or uninstalling, deletes this data.

3. Location

Location permission is requested only when you tap “Use the current Wi-Fi” (Android requires it to provide the network name) or “Current position” to write your coordinates on a tag. The value obtained is used on the device to fill in the form; it is not kept beyond the content you choose to write, and is not transmitted.

4. What a tag you write contains

Content written to an NFC tag is not protected: anyone who has the tag can read it with a phone. Do not write confidential information on it, and keep a Wi-Fi password for a tag you keep at home.

5. Permissions requested

  • NFC: read and write tags.
  • Wi-Fi state: offer the current network name.
  • Precise location: see section 3; requested only on your action.
  • Network access and advertising ID: used only by the advertising SDK to show ads. No other part of the app needs a connection.

6. Advertising and third parties

The app shows banners provided by Google AdMob. To select and deliver these ads, the SDK may collect an advertising ID and standard technical data (device type, Android version, approximate IP address). Google processes this data under its privacy policy and its AdMob ads page.

For people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before the first ad. Declining personalised ads never prevents using the app: non-personalised ads are shown instead. You can change this choice at any time in Options → Manage my privacy preferences.

Apart from the advertising SDK, the app shares no data with third parties.

7. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children and is not intended for under-13s. It does not itself collect any personal data, apart from the diagnostic log you choose to share.

8. Your rights

Since the app's data is only on your phone, you control it directly: view it in the app, delete it in the options or by uninstalling. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can manage your preferences in the app and in Android settings (Google → Ads).

9. Changes

This policy may be updated if the app evolves. Any change will be published on this page, with the date above.
